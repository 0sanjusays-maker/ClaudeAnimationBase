# THE LAST BENCH — audio cue sheet

Frame-accurate, for the editor. Frames are 1-based (F001 = 0.000 s) at 24 fps; the film is 1200 frames / 50.000 s.
The built track is `assets/last_bench_audio.wav`, generated by `tools/build_audio.py`: the spoken lines in `assets/vo/`
plus every effect synthesised from scratch (fan, clock, pen, paper, page, glass, latch, thud, bell, heartbeat, gong), so
there is no sound library to license.

Rebuild after adding or replacing a voice file:

```
python3 tools/build_audio.py
node render.mjs --encode --audio=assets/last_bench_audio.wav --out=out/last_bench.mp4
```

## Mix notes

- **The voices are the spine.** Teacher: dry, hoarse, 42 going on 50, never shouts. Boy: 14, close and dry and a little
  annoyed, never a ghost voice, no reverb, no pitch shift. Two distances only — "far" for the first *Present, sir*
  (thinner, more room), "close" for the second.
- **Room tone runs until F349** (fan loop plus a tick every second). At F349 the fan stops and at F351 the clock stops.
  Those two stops are the loudest thing in the film, because they are the absence of something.
- **The two silences are cues.** F353–F361 (0.3 s) and F852–F864 (1 s). Nothing in them. No music, no room tone.
- **No music anywhere.** The rhythm is the pen tap and the fan.
- **The ending is three sounds**: bell (F1177), one giggle cut off mid-laugh (F1189), silence (F1200). The giggle must
  **cut**, not fade.
- **Red section (F649–F720, F1128–F1176)**: pen scratch and an ink sizzle under the writing; a 40 Hz sub from F1081; a
  heartbeat at F1130 and F1150; a deep gong on the arrival of the red text at F1152 with 3 s of decay. Keep the sub
  under -20 dB; it should be felt, not heard.

## Stem list for the mix

| stem | contents | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `room` | fan loop, clock ticks, distant bell, air | ends F351 |
| `foley_pen` | every pen tap (F088–F148, F184–F188, F232–F236, F280–F292, F620–F640) | the teacher's rhythm; the four-tap version in shot 7 must be audibly *slower* |
| `foley_bench` | the four bench taps F376–F400 | wood, not paper; polite, unhurried |
| `foley_paper` | page rustles, page turns F421/F577/F793, the fanned pages F1079 | dry, close |
| `foley_small` | glasses clicks, pen clink, ink drop, latch | these carry the close-ups |
| `dx_teacher` | all teacher lines | see line cues |
| `dx_boy` | all boy lines | "far" for F193, "close" for everything else |
| `fx_red` | ink sizzle, drop, sub, heartbeats, gong | the only place sub-bass is allowed |
| `fx_end` | door creak, latch, register thud, bell, giggle | the giggle is a hard cut |
