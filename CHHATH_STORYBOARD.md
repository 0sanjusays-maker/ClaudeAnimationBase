# PEHLA CHHATH — storyboard and cut sheet

**75.0 s · 1800 frames · 24 fps · 1080×1920 (9:16) · Madhubani**

Song: *Pehla Chhath, teen peedhiyon ka saath* — the film is cut **to the vocal**, not to the clock: every cut lands on a
phrase or on 84 bpm (0.714 s), so a track at 84 drops straight on. The picture is a **silent animatic** with foley
(brush, footsteps, water) so the song stays the hero; nothing here is lip-synced and nothing is captioned except the
closing card.

Built film: `out/chhath_animatic.mp4` · scene code: `src/scenes/chhath.js` · sound: `CHHATH_AUDIO_CUES.md`

## The one motif

**bindu → sooraj → soop → diya → sooraj.** It opens the film, it fills it, and it closes it:

| when | the motif |
|---|---|
| 0.00 | a red **bindu** on cream paper — the only thing on screen |
| 0.4–1.6 | the bindu grows rings and rays: it becomes the **sun** |
| 1.6–2.5 | the sun's rim becomes the winnowing **soop**; the rays fold into its weave |
| 2.5–3.6 | a **diya** lights inside the soop; camera pushes into the flame |
| 20–34 | the soop is carried to the ghat with the sun going down behind it |
| 34–46 | the soop is lifted to a **sun** that is setting into the river |
| 46–49 | the sun goes under, and is **match-cut into a diya flame** |
| 49–70 | night, then the same walk at dawn, the **soop** lifted to the rising **sun** |
| 74.8 | the sun shrinks back into the red **bindu**; the border rubs out; **frame 1800 = frame 0** |

That last line is the reason the film is a loop: cutting the Reel back to the start is a cut back into its own first
frame, not a jump.

## The rhyme, in one line

Shots 4 and 8 are the **same walk, same spacing, same figures** — one at dusk going to the ghat, one at pre-dawn going
back. The colours change and nothing else does. That is the "teen peedhiyan" (three generations) idea, drawn.

## Characters

- **Gauri**, 15. Nervous for the first half: her brow is low, her hands are wrong on the soop, and she keeps checking
  her mother's face. From the arghya onwards she is calm. **Her whole arc is in the brow** — one line, lifted 3 px.
- **Maa**, 38. Steady. She never rushes anyone and never says anything; she corrects Pari's flower with two fingers.
- **Pari**, 7. Peeks from the doorway in shot 2, places a marigold crooked in shot 3, sleeps on her feet in shot 8, and
  gives the flower away in shot 9. **The flower is the film's only prop that moves between characters.**

## Style bible

Flat colour, **double outline** on every shape, patterned fill inside it, no shading, no gradients, no perspective.
Faces are near-profile with a **large almond eye** and a heavy brow; hands are the only detailed anatomy. Movement is
on twos except the water and the flame, which move every frame. **Only these animate: hands, eyes, dupatta, diya,
water, and the offerings.** Everything else — walls, doors, trees, borders — is locked and only boils.

Palette: cream paper `#F6EBD8` · ink `#211A16` · kumkum red `#B8291D` · vermilion `#D9532F` · marigold `#E5B45A` ·
saffron `#E9A93C` · leaf green `#3E6B4A` · indigo `#1B2547` · river `#25455C` · skin `#BE8A5C`.

**9:16 rule:** everything that matters sits inside x 70–1010, y 300–1640. Gauri's face and the soop are both inside the
centre 60% of the frame, so a Reel/Shorts crop never loses either.

### S1 · 0.0–4.0 · bindu to diya
Frame 0 is **only** the bindu and the paper — no wide shot, no establishing frame. The border then inks itself on,
one stroke per beat (0.06 → 3.4 s), which is the film's first "surprise": the frame is being drawn while you watch.
- 0.00–0.35 the bindu, alone.
- 0.35–1.55 rings and 30 rays grow out of it: the sun.
- 1.55–2.50 the sun's rim thickens and becomes the soop's rim; rays fold into the weave.
- 2.50–3.60 a diya lights inside the soop; the flame fills 40% of frame.
- 3.60–4.00 push into the flame; warm flare covers the cut.

### S2 · 4.0–10.0 · hands, then her eyes
Aangan, wall and floor. Gauri left, Maa right, the soop's rim in both their hands. Maa's hand comes over Gauri's
(4.45–5.25) and the fingers relax. **Pari peeks from the doorway** on the right. Camera tilts from the hands to Gauri's
face between 4.55 and 7.1 — slow, one move, no cut. Diya on the wall niche flickers (every frame); dupatta corner
moves (on twos).

### S3 · 10.0–18.0 · the soop fills
Locked, tight on the soop on a low stool with a red cloth. Six offerings, one per phrase, each placed by a hand
reaching in from the edge of frame — never by a full figure (a body at this scale reads as a pole):
`10.5 thekua · 11.7 bananas · 12.9 coconut · 14.1 fruit · 15.3 Pari's crooked marigold · 16.55 cane`.
Maa corrects Pari's flower with two fingers at 16.7. A marigold garland is strung across the top of frame, and the
cane arch grows up both sides over the eight seconds.

### S4 · 18.0–26.0 · the procession
Close on the soop in Gauri's hands, then the camera slides right (1.4–3.0) and the walk begins. Parallax: trees at 0.22×,
house at 0.45×, ground at 0.8×, walkers at 1×. Maa carries the **daura** on her head; Gauri the soop at her waist;
Pari holds her flower. Walking loop is two poses, 26 steps per shot.

### S5 · 26.0–34.0 · the ghat
Wide-ish, one slow drift. Sky in flat bands, the bank with steps and three floating diyas, the crowd as patterned
silhouettes. The three walk in from the left and stop by the steps at 32.2.

### S6 · 34.0–46.0 · the arghya — **the held shot**
Twelve seconds, one framing, a 4% drift and the sun sinking a hand's width. **Do not cut this.** Gauri stands in the
river (the water line crosses her at mid-thigh), the soop lifted, the setting sun behind it. Ripples leave her feet
every 1.5 s. Maa and Pari on the bank, hands folded. One fish, one lotus, moving slower than the water.
The emotion is carried by: her brow finally lifting, the soop not shaking, and the frame being still.

### S7 · 46.0–52.0 · the match cut
46.0–48.4: the sun sinks until the river cuts it. 48.4: **hard cut to black-red for four frames**, then the night,
where the **diya's flame sits exactly where the sun was**. Stars arrive in a Madhubani grid, one per beat, 49.1–50.7.
This is the film's only hard cut.

### S8 · 52.0–60.0 · the same walk, pre-dawn
Same composition and same spacing as S4 — trees, house, path, low wall — with indigo instead of amber. Pari walks
half asleep, still holding Maa's hand and still holding her flower. Nothing else changes: that is the point.

### S9 · 60.0–70.0 · usha arghya
The sun comes up instead of down. Same stance as S6, but Gauri is smiling. Rays on the water turn into lotus and
fish-tail patterns. **66.6: Pari gives her flower to Gauri. 68.3: Gauri puts it in the soop, off-centre.** Do not tidy
it — a child placed it.

### S10 · 70.0–75.0 · the three, the card, the loop
All three front-on and large, the sun low behind them, the soop and diya in front. 72.0–73.5 the card wipes on:
**छठ — आस्था जो पीढ़ियों से बहती है** (Tiro Devanagari, painted as a hand line, in kumkum red). 73.95–74.78 the frame
returns to bare paper, the border rubs out, and the **red bindu** is left — the same drawing as frame 0.

## Delivery notes

- **Loop:** the last frame equals the first. Do not add a fade to black; do not hold on the card.
- **Cut on the phrase, never on a second.** Every cut time above is also a beat at 84 bpm; if a vocal phrase runs long,
  move the cut later by whole beats (0.714 s), never earlier.
- **First 25 seconds are the fast half** — six cuts in 25 s, then one 12-second hold. If it feels slow early, shorten
  S3's offering gaps, not S6.
- **Nothing is captioned.** The card at the end is the only written word in the film.
